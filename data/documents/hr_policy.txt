Company HR Policy

Employee Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 18 paid leaves per year.

Employees can apply for casual leave, sick leave, and earned leave.

Casual leave can be used for personal requirements.

Sick leave can be used when an employee is unwell.

Employees should apply for leave through the company leave management system.

For planned leave, employees should submit their request at least 3 working days in advance.

Emergency leave should be communicated to the reporting manager as soon as possible.

Leave approval is subject to manager approval and business requirements.
