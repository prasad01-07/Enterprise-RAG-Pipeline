Company Information

The Enterprise RAG Pipeline is an internal company knowledge assistant.

The system is designed to answer questions about company documents.

Employees can use the system to retrieve relevant information from internal documents.

The system combines semantic search and keyword search to find relevant information.

ChromaDB is used for vector storage.

BM25 is used for keyword-based retrieval.

Ollama is used for local language model generation.

The system is intended to provide answers based only on the information available in the company's documents.
